## Opening a Ticket

If the solutions provided in this troubleshooting guide do not solve your problem, please reach out to get additional help.

Do not hesitate to reach out for requesting additional information about the plugin or guidance on particular features as well !

You can reach out through:

- [**GitHub issues**](https://github.com/MaelImhof/obsidian-jupyter/issues)
- [**Discord server**](https://discord.gg/KgkwwRJ3mQ)
  Join the Discord server and ask your questions in the home channel.

Your feedback and bug reports will help improve the plugin, thank you !
